Welcome to Na Hoku Secure Wi-Fi

If this is your first time connecting your iPhone to the NHStores wireless network, please install the Wi-Fi configuration profile.
⬇ Download Wi-Fi Profile
Estimated setup time: Less than 1 minute

Installation Instructions

  1. Tap Download Wi-Fi Profile.
  2. When prompted, tap Allow to download the Wi-Fi configuration profile.
  3. After the download is complete, tap Close.
  4. Open the Settings app.
  5. Tap Profile Downloaded near the top of the screen. If you don't see this option, go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and select the downloaded profile.
  6. Tap Install.
  7. Enter your iPhone passcode if prompted.
  8. Tap Install again to confirm.
  9. Once the installation is complete, your iPhone will automatically connect to the secure NHStores wireless network whenever it is available.
Important

• The Wi-Fi profile only configures your device to connect to the secure NHStores wireless network.

• You may be prompted to authenticate with your Na Hoku Microsoft account the first time you connect.

• After the profile is installed, your iPhone will automatically reconnect whenever the NHStores network is available.
